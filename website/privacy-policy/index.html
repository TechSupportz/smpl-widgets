Privacy Policy

Effective July 13, 2026

smpl. is designed to do its work on your device. We do not require an account, serve advertising, track you across apps, or operate servers that receive your calendar events, selected photos, location history, or widget preferences.

Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the smpl. iOS app and its widgets, together called “the App.” Information processed only on your device is not collected by us.

Calendar access

With your permission, the Events widget reads calendar names and upcoming event details so it can display them. The information is processed on your device and is not sent to us.

Selected photos

The Image widget uses Apple’s system photo picker. The App can access only the images you explicitly select through the picker and does not request access to your full photo library. Resized copies are stored on your device for the widget.

Location and weather

With your permission, the Weather widget uses the location you allow—precise or approximate—to request a local forecast from Apple WeatherKit. The request is made through Apple’s framework; we do not receive it on our own servers.

The most recent location is cached in the App’s shared on-device storage so the widget can remain reliable between refreshes.

Widget preferences

Widget appearance, selected calendar identifiers, cached purchase status, and other settings are stored locally in an App Group shared by the App and its widget extension.

App Store purchases

If you unlock premium widgets, Apple processes the purchase through the App Store. We do not receive your payment-card details. The App checks Apple’s StoreKit service and saves a local entitlement state so purchased features remain available.

Apple processes information under its own Privacy Policy.

Storage and deletion

  • Saved Image widget photos remain on your device until you delete them in the App or remove the App.
  • The cached location remains locally until it is replaced or the App is removed.
  • Widget preferences remain until changed, reset by the App, or removed with the App.
  • Calendar events remain with your calendar provider; the App does not create a server-side copy.

Deleting the App generally removes its local container and widget data, subject to iOS backups and Apple’s retention practices. Purchase history remains linked to your Apple Account so purchases can be restored.

Your controls

  • Change Calendar and Location permissions at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Choose Approximate Location instead of Precise Location.
  • Select individual images without granting full photo-library access.
  • Delete saved Image widget photos from within the App.
  • Remove the App to delete its remaining local App data.

Because we do not maintain user accounts or a user-data server, we generally do not hold personal data that can be retrieved or deleted on your behalf.

Website hosting

This privacy-policy page does not set cookies, run analytics, or include advertising. It is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process ordinary network data, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the page under its Privacy Policy.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App’s features or privacy practices change. The latest version will appear on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will be communicated in the App or through the App Store when appropriate.
